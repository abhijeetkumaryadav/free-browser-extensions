Article to EPUB

One-click web article exporter

Heuristic extraction — best effort. Text is pulled with simple page heuristics; complex layouts may export partially. The file is generated locally — nothing is uploaded.

How it works

On click, the extension reads the active tab and heuristically picks the article container (<article>, <main>, or the largest text block), splits it into paragraphs, and assembles a valid minimal EPUB (mimetype, container.xml, OPF, NCX, one XHTML chapter) entirely in your browser. No network calls, no accounts, no tracking.
