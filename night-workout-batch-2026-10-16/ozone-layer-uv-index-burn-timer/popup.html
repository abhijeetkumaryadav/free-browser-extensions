The ozone layer

Stratospheric ozone (~15–30 km up) absorbs most of the sun's UV-B and UV-C. When the ozone layer is thinner — e.g. the Antarctic spring hole — more UV-B reaches the surface, so the same weather can carry a higher UV index.

Sunscreen notes

  • SPF 30 theoretically multiplies unprotected burn time ×30 — if applied at 2 mg/cm². Most people apply far less.
  • Reapply every 2 hours, and after swimming or sweating.
  • Seek shade at peak UV (roughly 10:00–16:00).
  • No sunscreen blocks 100% of UV — shade and clothing come first.
