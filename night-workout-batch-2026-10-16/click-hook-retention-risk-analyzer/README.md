# Click Hook Retention — Self Scorecard

A creator self-scorecard for video hooks. **Your ratings — not AI analysis.**

## What it does
- Rate your video's hook on six criteria (Clarity, Curiosity gap, Payoff promise,
  Pacing, Visual hook, Retention setup) with 1–10 sliders.
- Live self-scored total out of 60 with an honest grade ("Strong hook — ship it"
  down to "Weak hook — likely to lose viewers early").
- Save scorecards per video title with notes; history, per-item delete, clear-all.

## Honest scope
This extension **cannot analyze videos**. It never watches or scores footage —
every number is one you chose yourself. It is a structured self-review tool.

## Privacy
100% local. Scores live in `chrome.storage.local`. Zero network.

## Build
Manifest V3, `storage` permission only.
