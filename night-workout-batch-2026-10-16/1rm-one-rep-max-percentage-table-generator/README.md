# 1RM Table — One-Rep-Max Calculator

Enter the weight you lifted and the reps you managed; the extension estimates
your one-rep max with **two classic formulas** and builds a 50–100% training
table (5% steps).

- **Epley:** `weight × (1 + reps/30)`
- **Brzycki:** `weight / (1.0278 − 0.0278 × reps)`
- kg / lb toggle, per-row copy, copy-whole-table button
- Last inputs persist locally in `chrome.storage.local`

**Honest scope:** formulas produce *estimates* — not guarantees. This is **not
medical advice**. Warm up, use a spotter, and consult a qualified coach or
clinician before max-effort lifting. 100% local — no network, no tracking.
