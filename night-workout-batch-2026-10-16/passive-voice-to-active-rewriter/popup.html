Passive Pattern Flagger

Rule-based writing helper

Honest scope: this flags likely passive-voice patterns using rules — it is not AI and it never rewrites for you. You do the rewriting.
How to rewrite (guidance)
  • Find the doer. Ask "by whom?" — "The report was written by Ana" → "Ana wrote the report."
  • Move the doer to the front. Subject first, then a strong verb.
  • Some passives are fine. When the receiver matters most ("The patient was treated"), keep it.
  • Watch "is/are/was/were + verb-ed". That's the most common signal.
