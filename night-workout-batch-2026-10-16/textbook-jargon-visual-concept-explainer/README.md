# Concept Canvas — Visual Glossary (v1.0.0)

A personal visual glossary for learning jargon: your terms, your definitions, your doodles.

## What it does

- **Terms** — search and tag-filter your glossary; view, edit, or delete entries.
- **Add** — create a term with a definition in your own words, a category tag, and a **doodle**: place rectangles, circles, and text labels on a canvas and drag them into a simple diagram.
- **Review** — flashcard mode: see the term and its doodle, flip to reveal your definition, navigate and shuffle.

## Honest scope

**Your notes only — does NOT explain textbook content.** There is no AI, no dictionary lookup, no textbook parsing. You add every term, definition, and doodle yourself. Definitions you write are study aids, not authoritative explanations.

## Privacy

100% local. Data lives in `chrome.storage.local` on your machine. No network requests, no telemetry.

## Permissions

- `storage` — save your terms and doodles on this device.

## Compatibility

Chrome / Edge / Firefox / Opera (Manifest V3).
