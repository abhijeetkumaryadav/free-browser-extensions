Heuristic signals, not verdicts

Heuristic signals only. This tool cannot tell you if media is real or fake. No AI analysis is performed. Always verify with trusted sources.

Scans images, video & audio on the active tab — 100% local.

What to look for 👀

  • Lighting & shadows that don't match the scene's light direction.
  • Skin looks waxy, airbrushed, or lacks pores; teeth/hands look wrong.
  • Blinking is absent, too rare, or oddly rhythmic in video.
  • Lip-sync mismatch — mouth movement doesn't match the audio.
  • Warped backgrounds — straight lines bend near faces.
  • Voice sounds flat, robotic, or the emotion doesn't fit the words.
  • Source check — unknown account, no original context, sensational caption.
  • Reverse image search the frame (links below) to find the original.

Signals are simple local heuristics (sizes, URLs, metadata). They can miss real fakes and flag innocent media. Nothing leaves your device.
