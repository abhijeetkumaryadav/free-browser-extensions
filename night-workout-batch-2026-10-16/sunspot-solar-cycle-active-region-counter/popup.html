Sunspot & solar-cycle guide
  • Wolf number: R = 10g + s — the classic daily sunspot index (Rudolf Wolf, 1848).
  • Solar cycle: ~11 years. We are in Cycle 25 (began Dec 2019), near its maximum — expect counts of 100+ on active days.
  • Groups vs spots: a cluster = 1 group; count each dark umbra as 1 spot.
  • Observe safely: never look at the Sun directly — use a filtered telescope or solar-projection only.
