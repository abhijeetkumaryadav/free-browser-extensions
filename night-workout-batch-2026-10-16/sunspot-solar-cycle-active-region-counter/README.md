# Sunspot Counter — Solar Cycle Logbook

**Educational only — your observations, no live solar data.**

- **Guide:** Wolf number formula (R = 10g + s), Cycle 25 notes, safe-observing
  warning (never look at the Sun directly — filtered telescope or projection only).
- **Log:** record a date, group count (g) and spot count (s) → your Wolf number,
  shown live as you type. History list with per-entry delete.
- **Chart:** bar chart of your last 30 Wolf numbers with the max labeled.

All data stays in `chrome.storage.local`.

## Permissions
- `storage` — save your observations.
