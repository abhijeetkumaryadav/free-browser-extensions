Emergency codes

7500

Unlawful interference

Hijack — controller initiates special handling and may ask "verify you are squawking 7500".

7600

Radio failure

Lost comms — follow radio-failure procedures (routes, altitudes, timing) for your airspace.

7700

Emergency

General emergency — declares an urgent situation; ATC gives priority handling.
