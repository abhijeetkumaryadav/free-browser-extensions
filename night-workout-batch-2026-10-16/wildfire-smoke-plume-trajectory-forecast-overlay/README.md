# Smoke Safety — Wildfire Notes

Educational wildfire-smoke tool. **No live forecast**: nothing is fetched from the network.

## What it does
- **Learn tab**: plain-language explainer — how smoke travels, why nights/mornings are worst, wind shifts, PM2.5, and practical steps on smoky days.
- **My Log tab**: log personal smoke observations (level 0–5, optional wind, notes) with stats (count, latest, 7-day average) and clearable history.

## Honest scope
Educational only — not a live forecast. Never rely on this extension for emergency decisions; follow local fire and health authorities.

## Privacy
Everything is stored in `chrome.storage.local`. No accounts, no network requests.

## Permissions
- `storage` — persists your observations on this machine.
