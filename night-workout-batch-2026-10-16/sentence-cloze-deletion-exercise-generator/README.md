# Cloze Generator — Fill-in-the-Blank Study Aid

Turn any text you paste into a fill-in-the-blank quiz. Two modes:

- **Blank every Nth word** — automatic, adjustable from 2 to 20.
- **Pick words myself** — tap any word to blank or unblank it, then start the quiz.

In the quiz, type the missing words, **Check answers** to score yourself, or **Reveal** to see the answers.

## Honest scope

Study aid only. It blanks words and scores typed answers — it does not judge grammar, meaning, or learning progress.

## Privacy

Fully local. Your text is kept in the extension's own storage (to restore your last paste) and is never sent anywhere.

## Files

- `manifest.json` — MV3 manifest, `storage` permission only
- `popup.html` / `popup.css` / `popup.js` — the whole app
- `icons/` — generated PNG icons (16/48/128)
