# Repurpose Planner — Cross-Platform Script Generator

Turn one idea into platform-ready script **outlines**: short-form hooks, carousel
slides, long-form beats, and a newsletter structure.

## What it does

- Enter a **topic** + **key points** (one per line).
- Click **Generate outlines** to fill the four platform templates.
- **Copy outline** per tab for pasting into your editor of choice.
- **Edit the templates** (gear tab) with placeholders:
  `{{topic}}`, `{{point1}}`…`{{pointN}}`, `{{allpoints}}`.
- Templates and inputs persist locally between sessions.

## Honest scope

- This is a **template-based planner, NOT AI generation**. It never writes
  sentences for you — it arranges *your* words into platform formats.
- No network. All data stays in `chrome.storage.local`.

## Permissions

- `storage` — persist your topic, points, and edited templates.

## Privacy

100% local. Nothing leaves your browser.
