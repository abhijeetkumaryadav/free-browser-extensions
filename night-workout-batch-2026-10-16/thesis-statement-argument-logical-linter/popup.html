Claim signal

A thesis usually stakes one clear claim. We look for a verb phrase carrying a position (argue, should, because) — absence of any may mean the statement reads like a fact list.

Evidence markers

Words like "studies show", "data", "for example" signal that support is promised. Missing them doesn't mean the thesis is wrong — it may mean the support lives elsewhere.

Hedge-word density

"Might", "perhaps", "possibly" soften claims. A few are fine; a thesis built mostly of hedges may lack conviction.

Length check

Very short statements (under ~12 words) rarely carry a full argument; very long ones (over ~60 words) may hide the claim.
