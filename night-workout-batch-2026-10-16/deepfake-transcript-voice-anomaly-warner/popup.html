Deepfake Awareness

1.0

Educational only — not detection. This guide teaches what to listen for; it cannot detect deepfakes.

Unnatural cadence

  • Robotically even pacing — no natural hesitations, breaths, or emphasis shifts.
  • Odd pauses mid-phrase, or sentences that run on without breath.
  • Emotion that does not match the words (flat voice on urgent claims).

Audio artifacts

  • Metallic or watery smearing on sibilants ("s", "sh") and plosives ("p", "b").
  • Voice timbre that wavers or shifts character mid-sentence.
  • Background noise that cuts out unnaturally under the voice, or echo that does not match the room.

Source & provenance checks

  • Where did the clip first appear? Re-uploads strip context and metadata.
  • Is there a second independent source reporting the same claim?
  • Would the speaker plausibly say this, in this setting, to this audience?
  • Check the account's history: new or repurposed accounts are a red flag.

Verify before you share

  • Pause: viral outrage clips are the most faked format.
  • Search the claim's exact wording — debunks often exist already.
  • If it would change your vote, your money, or your fear — check twice.
