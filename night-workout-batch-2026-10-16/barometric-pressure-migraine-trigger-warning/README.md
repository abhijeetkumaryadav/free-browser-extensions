# Pressure & Migraine Journal

A Chrome/Edge/Firefox/Opera MV3 extension for tracking barometric pressure
readings alongside migraine episodes, with a simple personal pattern view.

## Honest scope

- **Your logs — not medical advice.** This is a journal, not a diagnosis tool.
  Always discuss migraine management with your clinician.
- **No live data.** Barometers aren't in browsers; log readings from your own
  barometer, weather app, or station.
- Zero network, `storage` permission only.

## Features

- Log pressure (hPa) + episode severity (none/mild/moderate/severe) + notes.
- Pattern view (last 30 days): average pressure on episode vs non-episode days,
  plus episode rates on falling / rising / steady pressure.
- History with per-entry delete and clear-all.

## Install

1. Open `chrome://extensions` (or your browser's equivalent).
2. Enable Developer mode.
3. "Load unpacked" → select this folder.

## Privacy

Everything stays in your browser. No accounts, no network, no tracking.
