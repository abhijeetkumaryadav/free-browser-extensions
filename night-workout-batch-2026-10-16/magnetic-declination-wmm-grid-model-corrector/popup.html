How declination works
  • True north is the geographic North Pole; magnetic north is where a compass needle points. They differ by the magnetic declination, which changes by location and slowly over time.
  • East declination: magnetic north lies east of true north. To go from a true bearing to a magnetic bearing you subtract east declination.
  • Memory aid: "East is least" — subtract east declination when going true → magnetic. "West is best" — add west declination.
  • This tool is a pure math helper. It does not know your real declination — find it from an up-to-date magnetic declination chart.
