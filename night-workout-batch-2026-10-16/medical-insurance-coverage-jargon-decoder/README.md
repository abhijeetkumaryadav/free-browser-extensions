# Insurance Jargon Decoder

A personal glossary of health-insurance terms written in plain language. Search 30 bundled definitions, and add your own terms with your own definitions.

## What it does

- **Built-in glossary** — 30 common insurance terms (deductible, copay, coinsurance, out-of-pocket maximum, PPO, HMO, HSA, EOB…) explained in plain language.
- **Search** — instant filtering across terms and definitions.
- **Your terms** — add, edit, and delete your own entries. Stored in `chrome.storage.local`; nothing leaves your machine.

## What it does NOT do

> **Definitions only — not insurance advice.** This extension explains terminology. It does not recommend plans, interpret your coverage, or assess your situation. For coverage questions, talk to your insurer or a licensed advisor.

## Privacy

100% local. No network requests, no analytics, no accounts. Permissions: `storage` only.

## Install

1. Open `chrome://extensions` (or `edge://extensions`), enable Developer mode.
2. Click **Load unpacked** and select this folder.
3. Click the toolbar icon to open the glossary.
