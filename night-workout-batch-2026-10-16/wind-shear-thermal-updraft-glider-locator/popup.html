Thermal & Soaring Guide

Thermals · Wind shear · Cloud streets

Educational only — your notes. Not live weather. Always check official aviation weather before flying.

Thermals

Sun-warmed ground heats the air above it. That bubble of warm air rises — a thermal. Glider pilots circle inside it to gain altitude, then glide to the next one.

Wind shear

Wind shear is a sudden change of wind speed or direction with height. Near the ground it can make landings tricky — thermals often release where wind shifts.

Cloud streets

When thermals line up under rolling horizontal air, cumulus clouds form in long parallel rows — cloud streets. Fly along one and climb for kilometres without circling.

Remember

This guide teaches concepts only. Real soaring needs training, briefings and live conditions — none of which this extension provides.
