# Thermal & Soaring Guide

Browser extension for glider/soaring enthusiasts — educational concept guides plus a personal site-and-thermal notes log with ratings.

## What it does

- **Learn tab** — plain-language explainers: thermals, wind shear, cloud streets.
- **My Sites tab** — log flying sites: name, thermal notes, 1–5 star rating.
- Stats: site count, average rating, top site. Search across names/notes.
- All data stays in `chrome.storage.local`. Nothing leaves your browser.

## Honest scope

**Educational only — not live weather.** This extension fetches no weather data and cannot tell you where thermals are. It teaches soaring concepts and stores *your own* notes. Always check official aviation weather and fly with proper training.

## Permissions

- `storage` — save your site log locally.
