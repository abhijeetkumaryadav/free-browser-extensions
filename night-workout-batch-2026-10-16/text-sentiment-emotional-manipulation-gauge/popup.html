What the scanner counts, in plain language:

Sentiment score

Weighted positive/negative word lists. Score = (positive − negative) hits per 100 words, normalized to −100…+100.

Loaded language

Emotionally charged adjectives and nouns (e.g. "outrageous", "treacherous"). Rhetorical heat, not proof of manipulation.

False urgency

Time-pressure phrases ("act now", "before it's gone") that push a decision faster than the facts allow.

Fear / threat cues

Loss and danger words ("catastrophe", "you'll lose") that can bypass calm reasoning.

Flattery / in-group praise

Warmth aimed at you ("smart people like you") — lowers skepticism before the ask.

Shouting style

Exclamation density and ALL-CAPS sentences. Raised voice, not raised evidence.
