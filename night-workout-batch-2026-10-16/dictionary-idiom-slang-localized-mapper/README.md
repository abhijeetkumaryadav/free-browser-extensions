# Idiom Mapper

Your personal idiom & slang dictionary — a private notebook for phrases,
their meanings, and your own localized translations.

> **Honest scope:** YOUR entries only. This extension is **not** a real
> dictionary. There is no dictionary API, no lookup service, and no
> machine translation. Everything shown is text you typed yourself.

## Features

- **Browse** — search across phrases, meanings, and tags; filter by tag chip; delete entries.
- **Add** — save a phrase/idiom/slang with:
  - meaning in your own words,
  - your localized equivalent / translation,
  - comma-separated tags.
- **Review** — flip-card review deck (click or Space to flip, ←/→ to move, Shuffle).
- **Export** — download all entries as JSON.
- 100% local: entries live in `chrome.storage.local`. Zero network calls.

## Permissions

- `storage` — persist your entries locally. That's it.

## Install (developer mode)

1. Open `chrome://extensions` (or `edge://extensions`).
2. Enable **Developer mode**.
3. Click **Load unpacked** and select this folder.
