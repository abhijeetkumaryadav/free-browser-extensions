# Page Text Typo Scavenger Hunt

A user-triggered game: scan the current tab for likely misspellings, then click
the numbered markers to reveal the words and score.

**How to play:** click the extension, pick a time limit, hit *Start hunt on this tab*.
Numbered chips appear over words the heuristic flags as typos. Click a chip to
reveal the word (+10 points). Three hints pulse a random remaining marker.

**Honest scope:** typo detection is a heuristic against a bundled common-word
list — best-effort, it's a game, not a spellchecker. The page is **never edited**:
all markers, the HUD, and panels live on one removable overlay. Esc or *Quit*
removes every trace. 100% local, zero network, no remote libraries.
