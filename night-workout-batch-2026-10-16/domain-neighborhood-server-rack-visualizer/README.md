# Server Rack Visualizer

A playful, fully local server-rack doodle of the websites you save yourself.

## What it does

- Add sites (name + URL) to your personal rack — each becomes a rack unit on a canvas with blinking LEDs.
- Click a rack unit to open its site in a new tab.
- Sites list with per-site remove; all data stays in `chrome.storage.local`.

## What it does NOT do

- **Your sites only — cosmetic doodle.** Nothing here measures, probes, or reports on servers or domains. No network requests of any kind are made; the LEDs are decorative.

## Permissions

- `storage` — saves your site list on this device only.

## Privacy

Zero network. Everything stays on your device.
