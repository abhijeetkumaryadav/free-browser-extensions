Reading the sea

  • Significant wave height (Hs) — average of the highest third of waves. Individual waves can reach ~2× Hs.
  • Period — seconds between wave crests. Long period (10s+) = swell that traveled far; short period = local chop.
  • Direction — where waves come from, in degrees.
  • Swell vs wind waves — swell is smooth and long-period; wind waves are steep, short-period, and whitecapped.
