# Hydration Drink Logger

A simple daily logbook for water and electrolyte drinks. Log amounts in milliliters, see today's totals split by drink type, undo or clear entries, and review the last 7 days.

## Honest scope

**You log — NOT a balance estimate, NOT medical advice.** The extension cannot measure your hydration, electrolytes, or health. It only totals the amounts you enter. Talk to a healthcare professional about nutrition or hydration concerns.

## Permissions

- `storage` — your drink log, stored locally

Everything runs locally. Zero network.
