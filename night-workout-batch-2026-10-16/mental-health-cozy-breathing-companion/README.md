# Breathing Companion

A cozy guided breathing pacer with an animated circle, three rhythm styles, session tracking, and a private mood check-in log.

## What it does

- **Guided breathing** — animated circle with phase labels (Inhale / Hold / Exhale) and per-second countdowns.
- **Rhythms** — Boxed (4·4·4·4), 4-7-8, or Custom (your own inhale/hold/exhale seconds).
- **Sessions** — completed rounds are counted and persisted across visits.
- **Mood check-in** — log how you're feeling (1–5) with an optional note. Newest-first history, deletable. **No analysis is performed** — it is your own private record.

## What it does NOT do

> This is a relaxation and journaling tool, **not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment**. It makes no health claims. If you're struggling, consider reaching out to a professional or a helpline.

## Privacy

100% local. No network requests, no analytics, no accounts. Permissions: `storage` only.

## Install

1. Open `chrome://extensions` (or `edge://extensions`), enable Developer mode.
2. Click **Load unpacked** and select this folder.
3. Click the toolbar icon to open the companion.
