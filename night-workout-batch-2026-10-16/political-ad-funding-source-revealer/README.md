# Political Ad Funding Guide

An educational extension about **who funds political ads**, plus a personal log of ads
you've seen.

## Learn tab
- Who funds political ads: candidates & parties, PACs, Super PACs, 501(c)(4) "dark money"
  groups, individuals & companies
- How to read the "Paid for by …" disclaimer
- Where to verify: public ad libraries (Meta, Google)

## My ad log tab
- Log ads you see: platform, stated sponsor, your notes
- Search across your log, delete entries (cap 200)

## Honest scope
**Educational — your notes only.** No ad database, no verification, no network.
It stores only what you type. Rules vary by country and change over time; check
official sources and platform ad libraries for authoritative funding data.
