Political Ad Funding

Who pays for the ads you see

Who funds political ads?

  • Candidates & parties — run their own ads; usually the clearest "paid for by" disclaimers.
  • PACs — political action committees raising from members; contribution limits apply.
  • Super PACs — can raise and spend unlimited amounts, but may not coordinate with candidates.
  • 501(c)(4) "dark money" groups — nonprofits that can spend on politics without naming donors.
  • Individuals & companies — can fund ads directly, subject to platform and election rules.

Reading the disclaimer

  • Look for "Paid for by …" — the named funder is the legal sponsor.
  • "Not authorized by any candidate" = independent spending, not the campaign.
  • Vague names ("Citizens for a Better Tomorrow") often belong to PACs or nonprofits — the name itself reveals little.
  • Platforms (Meta, Google) keep public ad libraries where you can look up who paid and how much.

General educational info — rules vary by country and change over time.
