What these signals mean

  • Repetitive phrasing — the same sentence openers or phrases appear far more often than in typical human writing.
  • Low burstiness — sentence lengths barely vary; humans usually mix short punchy lines with longer ones.
  • Generic transitions — filler scaffolding ("Furthermore", "In conclusion", "It is important to note") used repeatedly.
  • Perfectly uniform tone — no slang, asides, or personality shifts across a long text.
