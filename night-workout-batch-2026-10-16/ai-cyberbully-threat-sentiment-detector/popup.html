BullyShield

Cyberbully threat detector

Heuristic tool, not AI. This scans visible page text against a bundled word-pattern list and flags possible bullying/threat signals. It can miss things and misfire. It is not a clinical or child-safety authority — always review flagged content yourself and talk to your child.

Runs only when you click. Text never leaves this device.

What to do next

  • Read the flagged messages in full context — tone and jokes matter.
  • Talk with your child before taking action; ask how it made them feel.
  • Save evidence (screenshots) if you plan to report to a school or platform.
  • Use the platform's block/report tools for repeat offenders.
  • If anyone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
