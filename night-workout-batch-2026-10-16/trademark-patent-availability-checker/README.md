# Trademark Search Helper

**What it does:** Type a candidate brand name → get direct links to the official
search pages of USPTO, EUIPO, and WIPO — and keep a local logbook of candidates
you've checked, with notes and dates.

**Honest scope (important):**
- This extension builds *links to official search pages* and stores *your logbook*.
- It does **NOT** check trademark availability, run any clearance search, or
  give legal advice. It is **NOT** legal clearance.
- Before committing to a name, run a proper search yourself and **consult a
  trademark attorney**.

**Privacy:** everything (logbook entries, notes) is stored locally in
`chrome.storage.local`. No network calls beyond opening official office pages in
new tabs. No accounts, no tracking.

**Permissions:** `storage` only.
