Why ownership matters

When a few companies own many outlets, editorial choices can reflect the owner's interests. Knowing who owns what is a basic media-literacy habit.

How to read ownership

  • Find the outlet's "About / ownership" page or parent company's annual report.
  • Follow the chain: outlet → publisher → parent company → controlling shareholders.
  • Note the parent company's other businesses — conflicts of interest hide there.

What "concentration" means

Concentration is how much of a market's audience or revenue sits with few owners. High concentration can narrow the range of viewpoints on offer.

Verify everything

Ownership changes (mergers, sales). Date your notes and re-check yearly.
