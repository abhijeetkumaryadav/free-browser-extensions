    Bot patterns to know

    Repetitive phrasingSwarm accounts reuse the same sentences with tiny tweaks. Identical or near-identical wording across many comments is the strongest tell.
    Generic praise"Great post!", "Amazing content!" — vague flattery that could apply to anything, often with emojis, no specifics.
    Emoji / link overloadHeavy emoji padding or links crammed into short text are classic amplification tactics.
    Short & content-freeVery short comments that say nothing substantive ("Nice!", "Wow 🔥") posted at volume.
    Timing clustersDozens of similar comments arriving within minutes — humans don't coordinate like that.
