How internet traffic actually works

  • Data travels as packets along undersea cables and fiber backbones — not as glowing arcs through space.
  • Packets hop through routers; each picks the next hop by destination, not by straight-line distance.
  • A "route" here is just two random dots on a drawing — real paths zig-zag through many intermediate networks.
