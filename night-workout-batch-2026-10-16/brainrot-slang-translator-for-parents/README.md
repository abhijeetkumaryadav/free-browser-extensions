# SlangSense — Brainrot Slang Translator for Parents

Decode what the kids are saying. Paste a message or scan the current page to
highlight youth slang terms with plain-language definitions from a bundled
40-term dictionary. 100% local — nothing is ever read, stored, or sent anywhere.

## Features

- **Paste text** tab: paste any message, get every slang term explained with an example.
- **Scan this page** tab: highlights slang on the active tab with hover definitions
  (one click, fully reversible with "Clear highlights").
- **Dictionary** tab: searchable full dictionary + suggest-your-own-term (stored only
  on this device; your terms take priority over bundled ones).

## Honest scope

The dictionary is informal, crowd-knowledge slang — definitions may be outdated,
regional, or just wrong. It's a conversation starter, not an authority. When in
doubt, ask your kid (they love explaining).

## Permissions (all justified)

- `storage` — your suggested terms only.
- `activeTab` + `scripting` — highlight slang on the current page only when you click
  "Scan this page". Page content is never read or transmitted.

## Install (developer mode)

1. Open `chrome://extensions`, enable Developer mode.
2. "Load unpacked" → select this folder.
