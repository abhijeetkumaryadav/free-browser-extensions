# Urban Foraging Plant Mapper

**Urban Foraging Plant Mapper** — a local reference of common edible urban plants with safety notes, plus your personal foraging log. 100% local, no maps, no network.

> ⚠️ **Safety:** Never eat anything you are uncertain about. Always verify with a local expert guide before foraging.

## What it does

- **Reference tab** — 12 common edible urban plants with identification notes, edible parts, season, and caution notes (standard published foraging knowledge, summarized).
- **My Log tab** — record your own finds: plant name, date, spot, notes. Edit and delete anytime. Stored in `chrome.storage.local`.

## Honest scope

- This is a **reference and personal log**, not a map of live foraging spots and not an identification app.
- Plant information is general guidance; local laws and seasons vary.

## Permissions

- `storage` — saves your foraging log locally.

## Install (developer mode)

1. Open `chrome://extensions`, enable **Developer mode**.
2. **Load unpacked** → select this folder.

## Design

380px popup, deep-dark glassmorphism, one amber accent (`#f59e0b`), Lucide-style stroke icons, generated 16/48/128 PNG icons.
