# Plate Math — Barbell Loading Visualizer

Enter your **target weight**, the **bar weight**, and the **plates available** at
your gym. The extension greedily computes plates per side (largest first) and
draws a color-coded barbell diagram.

- Plates per side listed with counts
- Visual barbell diagram — plate colors by size
- Remainder warning when the target can't be hit exactly (e.g. missing 2.5s)
- kg / lb toggle; settings persist locally

**Honest scope:** a math helper. Plate colors/availability vary by gym — **check
your gym's actual plates** before loading. Not medical advice. 100% local, no
network, no tracking.
