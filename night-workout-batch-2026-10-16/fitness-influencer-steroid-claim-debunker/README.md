# Claim Journal — Fitness Influencer Claims

A personal journal for logging fitness influencer claims you spot online — the claim, who said it, the source link, and your own notes. Tag each entry **Unchecked**, **Doubtful**, or **Verified by me**, and search/filter your history.

## Honest scope (read this)

- **Your notes only.** This extension logs what *you* record. It performs **no AI fact-checking** and gives **no medical advice**.
- "Verified by me" means *you* checked it, not that the extension verified anything.
- Nothing leaves your browser: all data lives in `chrome.storage.local`.

## Features

- Add claims with who / source link / notes and a tag
- Search across claim text, person, source, and notes
- Filter by tag (Unchecked / Doubtful / Verified by me)
- Delete entries; newest first; claim count

## Permissions

- `storage` — save your journal locally.

## Privacy

Fully local. No network, no analytics, no accounts.
