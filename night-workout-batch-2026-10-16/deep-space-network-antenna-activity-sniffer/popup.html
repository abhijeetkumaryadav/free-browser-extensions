Antenna facts

70 m dishes The giants: DSS-14 (Goldstone), DSS-43 (Canberra), DSS-63 (Madrid).
34 m BWG dishes Beam-waveguide workhorses at all three complexes.
Three complexes, ~120° apart Goldstone (California), Madrid (Spain), Canberra (Australia) — so one always faces deep space.
Why it matters The DSN talks to probes like Voyager, Perseverance and JWST — billions of km away.
