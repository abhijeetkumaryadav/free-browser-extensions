      PM2.5 quick guide

      PM2.5 is fine particulate matter ≤ 2.5 µm — the pollutant most often driving AQI near wildfires and cities. Rough mapping: 0–12 µg/m³ ≈ Good, 12.1–35.4 ≈ Moderate, 35.5–55.4 ≈ USG, 55.5–150.4 ≈ Unhealthy, 150.5–250.4 ≈ Very Unhealthy, 250.5+ ≈ Hazardous.

      What AQI means

      AQI compresses pollutant concentrations into one 0–500 index. Outdoor exercise guidance changes around band 101+ (sensitive groups) and 151+ (everyone). This is general information, not health advice — follow local health authorities on smoky days.
