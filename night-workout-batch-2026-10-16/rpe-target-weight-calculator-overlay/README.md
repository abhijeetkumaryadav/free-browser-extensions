# RPE Target Weight Calculator

Enter your one-rep max, target reps, and target RPE — get an estimated
training weight from the widely-published RPE percentage chart, plus the
full chart for reference.

**Scope (honest):** Estimate only — **not medical advice**. RPE charts are
population averages; individual variation is large. Train safely.

## Features

- Target weight = 1RM × chart percentage (RPE 6–10 × 1–12 reps)
- Full RPE chart rendered in the popup, with your row/cell highlighted
- Remembers your last 1RM locally

## Permissions

- `storage` — remembers your last 1RM

## Local-only

No network calls, no analytics. Everything stays in the browser.
