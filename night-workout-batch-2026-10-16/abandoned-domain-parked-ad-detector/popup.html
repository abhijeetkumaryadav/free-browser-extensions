Parked Domain Spotter

Red flags of abandoned / parked domains

Educational only — not detection. This tool cannot check whether a domain is parked; it teaches you what to look for.

Quick habits before you trust a site

  • Check the real address bar host — squatters use look-alike domains.
  • Try one link: parked pages usually have no working navigation.
  • Search the domain name plus "reviews" — squats rarely have any.
  • Parked pages almost never have real contact details or an about page.
