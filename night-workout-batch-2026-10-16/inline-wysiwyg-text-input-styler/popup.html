Markdown Toolbar

Inserts markdown — not rich text.

Honest scope: adds a small toolbar above the active text field that wraps your selection in markdown. Real WYSIWYG in arbitrary inputs isn't possible — pages show the raw markdown text. Nothing runs until you click.
Click Attach, then click a textarea or text input on the page. The toolbar appears just above it.
