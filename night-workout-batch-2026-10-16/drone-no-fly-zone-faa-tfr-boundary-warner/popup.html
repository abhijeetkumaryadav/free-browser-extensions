Airspace basics (general info)

  • Controlled airspace — around airports and busy areas. Drone flights there usually need prior authorization.
  • LAANC concept — in some countries, a system that lets drone pilots request near-real-time authorization for controlled airspace.
  • Common restrictions — military bases, national parks, stadiums during events, emergency response areas, critical infrastructure.
  • Height rules — many jurisdictions cap recreational flights (often ~120 m / 400 ft AGL); always confirm locally.
  • Line of sight — keep the drone in visual line of sight unless local rules allow otherwise.
