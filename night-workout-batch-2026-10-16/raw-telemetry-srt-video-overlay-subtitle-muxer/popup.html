Telemetry SRT Builder

Timed telemetry lines → .srt file

Subtitle generator — not video muxing. It produces a standard .srt text file from the lines you type, which you can add to a video in an editor.

Format hh:mm:ss,mmm — also accepts seconds (e.g. 75.5) or mm:ss.
