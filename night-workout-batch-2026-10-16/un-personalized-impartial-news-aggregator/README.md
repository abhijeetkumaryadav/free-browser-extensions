# My Feeds — RSS Reader

An RSS reader for feeds **you** choose.

**Honest scope:** this is not impartial and not a news service. Every headline
shown comes from an RSS feed you added yourself (a few well-known starters are
preloaded; keep or remove them freely). Add any feed URL to expand your list.

## Permissions

- `storage` — remembers your feed list.
- host access (`http`/`https`) — fetches the RSS feeds you added, nothing else.
