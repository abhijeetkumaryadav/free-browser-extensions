Mixing order

  1. Silica first (if used) — mix well, wait 10 min
  2. Cal-Mag
  3. Base nutrient — Part A
  4. Base nutrient — Part B (never mix A & B concentrate together)
  5. Additives / boosters
  6. pH adjust last, after everything is mixed

Stir thoroughly between each addition. Always verify with a calibrated EC/pH meter — this tool gives estimates, not prescriptions. Start low with new plants.
