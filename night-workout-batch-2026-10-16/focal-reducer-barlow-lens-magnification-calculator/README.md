# Barlow & Focal Reducer Calculator

Enter your **telescope focal length**, **eyepiece focal length**, and an optional
**Barlow lens or focal reducer**, and get the effective system at a glance.

## What it does
- **Magnification** = (telescope focal × factor) ÷ eyepiece focal
- **Effective focal length** with the accessory applied
- **F-ratio** (if you enter aperture)
- **Exit pupil** (if you enter aperture)
- **True field of view** — estimated assuming a 50° apparent-field eyepiece
- Presets: Barlow 2×/3×/5×, reducers 0.63×/0.5×/0.8×, plus a custom factor (0.1–10×)

## Honest scope
**Textbook optics — estimates only.** Assumes thin lenses and ideal geometry.
Real Barlows and reducers change effective power with drawtube spacing, and
true field depends on your actual eyepiece's apparent field.

## Privacy
Fully local — inputs saved in `chrome.storage.local`. Zero network.
