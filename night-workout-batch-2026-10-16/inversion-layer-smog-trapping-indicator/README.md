# Inversion & Smog Guide

Browser extension explaining temperature inversions, lapse rates and why smog gets trapped, plus a personal observation log with notes and haze levels.

## What it does

- **Learn tab** — plain-language explainers: what an inversion is, lapse rates, why smog gets trapped.
- **My Log tab** — log sky observations: haze level (0–5), sky condition, free notes.
- Stats: observation count, 7-day average haze level, latest label. Search across notes.
- All data stays in `chrome.storage.local`. Nothing leaves your browser.

## Honest scope

**Educational only — not live data.** This extension fetches no air-quality or weather data and measures nothing about the air. It teaches inversion concepts and stores *your own* observations. Use official air-quality sources for real decisions.

## Permissions

- `storage` — save your observation log locally.
