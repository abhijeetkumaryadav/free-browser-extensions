What is an inversion?

Normally air cools with height. In an inversion, a layer of warm air sits above cooler air near the ground — the temperature rises instead of falling. The warm lid acts like a ceiling.

Lapse rates

The lapse rate is how fast temperature changes with height. Positive lapse = normal cooling (~6.5 °C/km). Negative lapse = inversion. When the rate flips near the surface, pollution has nowhere to rise.

Why smog gets trapped

Warm air is lighter — it won't sink through the warm lid. Traffic exhaust and haze stay under the inversion, concentrating near the ground. Valleys and clear, calm nights make inversions stronger; sunshine usually breaks them.
