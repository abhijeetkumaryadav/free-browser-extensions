# Interview Question Generator v1.0.0

Paste a job description and get a structured interview question bank:
behavioral, technical, situational, and culture-fit sections. The
on-device template engine spots skills, seniority, and experience
requirements and weaves them into the questions. Copy per question or
export all as Markdown.

**Template-based — no AI service, no network, everything stays on
your device.**

## Try it
1. Unzip `interview-question-generator.zip`.
2. `chrome://extensions` → Developer mode → **Load unpacked** → select folder.
3. Click the icon, paste a job description, **Generate question bank**.

## Permissions
- `storage` — remembers your last generated bank.
- `clipboardWrite` — copy buttons.
