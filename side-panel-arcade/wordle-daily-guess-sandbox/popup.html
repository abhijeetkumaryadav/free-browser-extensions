How to play

Guess the 5-letter word in 6 tries.

  • W — letter is correct and in the right spot.
  • O — letter is in the word but in the wrong spot.
  • R — letter is not in the word.

Daily mode: one word per day, seeded by the date. Practice mode: unlimited words.
