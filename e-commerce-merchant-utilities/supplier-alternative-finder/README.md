# Supplier Alternative Finder v1.0.0

Sourcing worksheet for comparing suppliers. Enter your product plus
current supplier terms (unit cost, MOQ, lead time), then add up to 5
alternative suppliers and compare them side by side — the best unit
cost, MOQ, and lead time are highlighted automatically. One-click
search launchers open Alibaba, Made-in-China, Thomasnet, and Google
with your product prefilled so you can find alternatives fast.

## Try it
1. Unzip `supplier-alternative-finder.zip`.
2. `chrome://extensions` → Developer mode → **Load unpacked** → select folder.
3. Click the icon, enter your product + terms, hit a launcher, add the suppliers you find.
