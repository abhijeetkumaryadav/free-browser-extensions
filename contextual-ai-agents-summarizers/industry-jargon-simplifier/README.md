# Industry Jargon Simplifier v1.0.0

Paste any text — an on-device glossary (business, legal, tech, medical
basics) highlights jargon terms. Click a term to see its plain-language
meaning inline, then hit **Simplify all** to produce cleaned text with a
copy button. Add your own terms to a persistent custom glossary.

Nothing leaves your device; all matching is local.

## Try it
1. Unzip `industry-jargon-simplifier.zip`.
2. `chrome://extensions` → Developer mode → **Load unpacked** → select folder.
3. Click the icon, paste text, and hit **Find jargon**.
